About QuickNotes

QuickNotes helps you capture ideas, reminders, and tasks in one simple place. Add notes so you can find and remember them later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type your note in the New note box.
  3. Select Add Note to add it to your notes.

Features

  • Add notes quickly.
  • Keep reminders in one place.
  • View your notes in a simple list.
